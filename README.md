## My Favorite Movie: *Four Sisters and a Wedding* 🇵🇭

**Four Sisters and a Wedding** is a Filipino family comedy-drama film released in 2013. It tells the story of four siblings who come together to stop their brother's wedding.

### Why I Like It
- Funny and heartwarming story
- Shows the value of **family** in Filipino culture
- Relatable characters and Filipino humor

### Main Cast
1. Toni Gonzaga
2. Bea Alonzo
3. Angeline Quinto
4. Shaina Magdayao
